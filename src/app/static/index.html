          How would you like to start?

          Has the other party already made an offer?

          Required. If yes, add notes (informal is fine), choose the offer document, or both. Your case cannot continue until at least one is provided.

          Evidence-grounded settlement planning

          Washington-only decision support. The calculations use your entered assumptions; they are not legal classifications, a predicted result, or legal advice. A Washington attorney or mediator should review the draft.

          Who operates this case

          This case belongs to you, one side only. You may invite one attorney or paralegal by email to operate it instead of you. Only one account can use the case at a time: while your delegate has access you cannot, and revoking access lets you resume immediately. The other party is never invited, never sees this workspace, and is not a recipient of offers from here. Delegation is your explicit permission only; it does not verify anyone's identity or legal authority.

              Separate consent for settlement planning

              Local calculations and manual scenario selection do not require AI consent. This purpose is separate from document extraction. If you consent, the configured AI provider receives only the data listed below to rank supplied scenarios. Consent can be revoked at any time.

              Active adopted facts

              Only adopted facts with an available source document can be used as evidence references. Copy a fact ID into scenario dates when it supports that assumption.

                Append ledger events

                Enter events as JSON. Money amounts are integer cents. Events are append-only; corrections require a new, clearly labeled event. Source-supported entries must cite an active fact ID. Do not enter bank credentials or account numbers.

                  [{"asset_id":"replace-with-uuid","asset_name":"Premarital savings","asset_kind":"asset","event":{"effective_date":"2020-01-01","sequence_number":0,"event_type":"acquisition","amount_cents":100000,"source_allocations":{"user_separate":100000,"other_separate":0,"community":0,"unresolved":0},"provenance":"user_reported","note":"Starting balance"}}]

                  Compare bounded scenarios

                  Choose explicit dates, tracing conventions, and proposed community shares. The engine does not presume a 50/50 division or decide CIR status.

                  After selection, the selected assumption set is copied into the active attempt. Editing that set locally recalculates only this attempt; it does not change case facts or the append-only settlement ledger, and it does not request AI ranking.

                  {"relationship_windows":[{"id":"marriage-only","label":"Marriage-only dates","relationship_type":"marriage_only","start_date":"2020-01-01","separation_date":"2024-01-01","source_fact_ids":[],"assumption_note":"User-provided dates"}],"tracing_methods":["explicit"],"community_share_bps":[5000]}

                  Prepare a CR 2A proposal draft

                  First Attempt — active and editable

                  Create editable Word and Excel drafts from the selected calculation and your proposed terms. The Word draft is unsigned and is not filed. A signed CR 2A agreement may have legal effect; review it with qualified Washington counsel before signing or sharing. Nothing is sent unless you explicitly confirm delivery.

                  Preview, editing, and proposal drafts are free. Checking this case's attempt-credit balance…

                  Milestones and proposed values below belong only to this negotiation attempt. They do not change source facts or the evidence-backed settlement ledger; a later attempt starts with a separate editable copy.

                  [{"name":"Refinance deadline","date":"2027-01-15"}]
                  [{"asset_id":"00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000001","proposed_amount_cents":12500000}]

                  Draft downloads above are free and keep this attempt editable. The first successful finalization for this case is free; each later successful finalization uses one attempt credit. Finalizing saves a snapshot of both sides' values and makes this attempt read-only. Finalizing does not mean you sent the files to anyone — attach and confirm what you actually sent separately below.

                  Optional: save or send generated drafts

                  These controls are separate from the response tracker. A delivery action explicitly sends the generated drafts only after confirmation; it does not send your later edits. To track the actual final offer, review and edit the files, send those final versions yourself, and attach the exact files below. The files are never sent to AI.

                                    Check any judgment and deadlines
                                    
                
                                    The signed decree or order and case docket control whether money-judgment obligations or deadlines apply. Not every case has a judgment, and a certificate of dissolution does not resolve a money judgment.
                                    
                
                                    No statewide Superior Court family-law form titled “Satisfaction of Judgment” or “Stipulation and Order Regarding Final Payment of Judgment to Respondent” has been verified for this workflow. The similarly named WPF GARN 01.1200 form is for garnishment, not a generic divorce judgment. Ask the Superior Court Clerk or consult official local family-law resources about any applicable document and procedure after a judgment is fully satisfied. If a judgment deadline may be missed, seek advice promptly; any agreed stipulation or order may require court approval or acceptance.
