Casework account notices

Version: 2026-11-09. These draft notices require qualified counsel review before public launch. They do not replace a complete, approved service agreement or privacy policy.

Terms

Casework helps organize case materials and prepare mediation proposals. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or representation. Review source information, calculations and drafts yourself and consult qualified counsel. No outcome, fairness or acceptance is guaranteed.

Use an account and materials you are authorized to use. Protect your sign-in credentials. Downloading a proposal is not delivery; you decide whether to provide it to your mediator. Casework does not automatically send it to the opposing party.

Selecting an agreement button records acceptance of this version of these account terms and acknowledgment of the privacy notice below. It is not consent to marketing or model training.

Privacy Notice

Email signup uses your email, first and last name, a securely hashed password, email-verification records and versioned agreement information. Email verification tokens expire after five minutes. Sessions and authentication events are used to operate and protect your account.

If you agree to continue with Google, Google authentication starts and we use the verified identity returned by Google to sign you in or create an account. Casework does not store Google's access or refresh tokens. Declining before Google authentication starts creates no account or Google connection. This does not revoke permissions you independently granted to Google or other services previously.

Case materials and saved work are used to provide the features you request. Closing a case is reversible and does not delete it. Account and case-data deletion controls are separate. Provider-managed database backups may have separate retention; this deployment does not verify their purge.

These account notices do not authorize use of your materials for model training. Any such use requires separate legal review and appropriate disclosures and consent.
