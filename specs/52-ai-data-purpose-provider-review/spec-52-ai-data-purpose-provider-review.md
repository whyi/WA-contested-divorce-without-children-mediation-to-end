- **Status:** Engineering safeguards implemented; provider evidence and qualified counsel review unavailable and outstanding. This spec records an evidence inventory and a blank review template, not a provider or legal approval.

- **Supersession:** Additive only. Historical specifications are unchanged. This spec adds purpose separation and production gates; it does not authorize any new provider, purpose, retention period, training use, or legal position.

## Objective and limits

Inventory what the current application sends to Amazon Bedrock for user-requested work, prevent either analysis consent from being treated as consent for generalized training or product improvement, and block production AI unless the existing overall readiness gate, separate provider-review evidence, and qualified-counsel evidence are recorded by operators.

This engineering work is not a legal opinion, provider contract review, counsel review, assurance about vendor behavior, or proof that a configuration flag is true in fact. No legal/provider review evidence was available when this spec was prepared. Do not mark the accompanying template complete, set either evidence flag on that basis, or close issue #128 until signed/current provider evidence and qualified-counsel evidence are supplied and retained.

## Requirements

- **FR-52-01 — Distinct purposes:** User-requested document analysis and settlement ranking retain their separate affirmative-consent events and notices. Neither consent authorizes provider retention, human review, product improvement, or generalized training.
- **FR-52-02 — No generalized training path:** Do not add a case-content training, product-improvement, fine-tuning, or evaluation export/integration under this spec. Generalized model training/product-improvement use of case content remains disabled in application behavior. No feature or flag here can enable it. Any future proposal requires separate issue/specification, verified provider and legal evidence, a distinct specific consent, and a new review before implementation.
- **FR-52-03 — Fail closed in production:** `AI_ANALYSIS_ENABLED` remains false by default. When production readiness is required and AI is enabled, readiness requires both `COUNSEL_REVIEW_EVIDENCE_VERIFIED=true` and `AI_PROVIDER_REVIEW_EVIDENCE_VERIFIED=true`, in addition to every other existing production readiness check. The flags have different owners/evidence and do not verify themselves. If any production readiness check fails, document-analysis availability, settlement-ranking availability, and worker processing are blocked.
- **FR-52-04 — Consent before provider call:** Provider-call helpers require an explicit consent assertion from their already-authorized caller in addition to configuration checks. The production worker independently validates the active request-specific consent and notice snapshot before invoking Bedrock. Settlement ranking requires its separate current consent before invoking Bedrock.
- **FR-52-05 — Current configuration and disclosure:** Require enabled AI, authenticated non-demo case/material access, configured Bedrock model and region, purpose-specific notice version where applicable, and a non-empty deployment handling notice. Show and snapshot the exact configured notice plus a fixed statement that this application does not independently verify vendor-managed retention, human access/review, improvement/training, subprocessors, or deletion behavior. A notice is not provider evidence.
- **FR-52-06 — No case-content logs:** Do not log prompts, document or extracted text, case facts, source quotes, user objectives, model responses, credentials, or provider error messages. Log only safe event identifiers and exception types. Deployment log/analytics providers remain separately subject to review.
- **FR-52-07 — Evidence flag scope:** `AI_PROVIDER_REVIEW_EVIDENCE_VERIFIED` defaults false. It may be changed only by the designated deployment operator after current, signed or otherwise verifiable provider evidence is reviewed and retained in the companion checklist. It is an operator assertion, not a cryptographic attestation, legal approval, or guarantee.
- **FR-52-08 — Historical specs:** Keep Specs 06, 08, 09, 32, 41, 50, and 51 unchanged. Their existing storage, consent, review, closure, mediator, readiness, and counsel boundaries continue to apply.

## Acceptance criteria

1. AI is disabled by default; production AI remains unavailable unless the entire production readiness gate passes and both distinct provider and counsel evidence flags are set.
2. Either missing evidence flag independently blocks production AI, with safe check codes that distinguish provider evidence from qualified-counsel evidence.
3. User-requested analysis still works only with its current explicit, purpose-specific consent, current provider/model/configuration and notice, plus the production readiness gate when required.
4. Analysis and settlement consent schemas reject extra training-consent claims; their consent events are not accepted as training consent.
5. No training/improvement payload or provider integration is added; test provider requests contain only their user-requested analysis data.
6. Tests verify case content and provider-response text are not exposed in AI-related logs.
7. The provider/counsel evidence template remains uncompleted until independently verified evidence is supplied; no unsupported retention, deletion, human-review, training, or legal statement is made.
